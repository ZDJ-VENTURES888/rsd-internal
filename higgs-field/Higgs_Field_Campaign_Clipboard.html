Video & Cinematic Story Arc
Universal three-beat structure for Reels, Stories, and Facebook Video. Say less on screen — let the imagery and sound do the emotional work.
Beat 1
Hook
0:00 – 0:04
No text or minimal text. Pure image. The corner of something beautiful — a sound, a texture, a feeling. One question in the viewer's mind: What is that? Curiosity is the only goal here.
Beat 2
Build
0:04 – 0:20
Slow pan or reveal. Ambient audio: water, breeze, fire crackle. One line of text overlay maximum — let the space sell itself. Resistance builds desire. No narration needed.
Beat 3
Dream Outro
0:20 – 0:30
Full scene reveal with family present. Life being lived. One line of copy max. CTA overlay appears. Higgs Field logo on dark field — 2 seconds alone. Silence or single piano note.

Tone & Sound Direction
Music
Warm, cinematic, instrumental. Slow piano, light acoustic guitar, or ambient electronic. No lyrics. No upbeat commercial energy. Feeling: premium, quiet confidence.
Color Grade
Golden hour warmth. Slight desaturation on shadows. Clean highlights. Real-world light — not oversaturated. Reference: architectural lifestyle photography.
Text Treatment
White or off-white text, 30-40% of screen width, bottom-third placement. One line at a time. Fade in/out. Never stacked. Never busy. Silence does the work.
Voiceover (60-sec only)
If used: male or female, measured pace, low register. No excitement. Conversational. As if speaking to one person, not broadcasting to many.
